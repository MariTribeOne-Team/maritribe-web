The founder

Shankar M.

LinkedIn ↗ Email

“I don’t want to end up simply having visited this world.” — Mary Oliver

That line sits at the top of my profile, and it is as good a place as any to explain why I built maritribe. I’ve spent my career solving for scale — carrying propane and butane across the seas to feed thousands of industries and millions of homes, and later helping a search engine connect thousands of businesses to the people looking for them. maritribe is that same instinct turned toward the people I know best: seafarers.

From the engine room to the product room

I started at sea. I’m a marine engineer by training (B.Tech, MIT Academy of Engineering), and I spent my early career with Anglo-Eastern and then Exmar Ship Management — responsible for a ship’s entire power generation, leading a 30-member international team, and helping debug and roll out in-house operational software across the fleet. Working alongside colleagues from eighteen nationalities is where my idea of a truly connected maritime community first took root.

When I came ashore, I joined Sea and Beyond — a firm built specifically to help seafarers transition into shore-based careers and find mentorship — before doing my MBA at IIM Ahmedabad (PGPX). From there I became Chief of Staff to the founder and CEO of the Enerji group, set up new service lines from zero to one, led product at Justdial, and today I’m a Senior Product Manager at GeoServe building a global fuel-trading platform.

Why I built maritribe

The maritime world runs the planet — and yet the people who keep it moving are scattered, underserved, and largely invisible to one another. Career paths are opaque, welfare conversations happen in private, and the knowledge that should flow freely between ports stays locked inside individual ships and companies. I’ve lived that gap from both sides of the gangway.

So my mission with maritribe is simple: to give maritime professionals a home of their own. A place as easy as WhatsApp and as powerful as LinkedIn — where a rating in training, a master at sea, a shore-side manager and a regulator can connect, learn, and grow together. The maritribeOne podcast is part of that mission: honest, on-the-record conversations with the people who actually run this industry, so the lessons stop disappearing over the horizon.
